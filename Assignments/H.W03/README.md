
## Case Study: AI Course Launch Failure
A company launched a new online AI course but received very few enrollments.
------------------------------
## Questions to Discuss:

   1. What digital marketing channels should the company use?
   2. How can AI improve the campaign?
   3. What metrics should be monitored?


-----------------

## 1. Digital Marketing Channels to Use
We need to go where the tech crowds hang out. Forget standard billboards; we need high-intent channels.

* Search Engine Optimization (SEO): Create blogs about "How to learn AI in 2026." This brings in free, organic traffic.
* LinkedIn Ads: This is gold for B2B. Target professionals like software engineers or data analysts who need to upskill.
* YouTube & TikTok: Post short, valuable clips. Show a 60-second tutorial on building an AI app, then link to the course.
* Online Communities: Don't spam, but be active on Reddit, Discord, and GitHub. Answer questions and mention the course naturally.

------------------------------
## 2. How AI Can Improve the Campaign
Since it is an AI course, we should absolutely use AI to sell it. It saves time and hyper-personalizes the marketing.

* Smart Content Creation: Use GenAI to write 50 different versions of ad copy and email subject lines in seconds.
* Predictive Targeting: Use Meta and Google’s AI algorithms to find lookalike audiences who actually buy tech courses.
* Dynamic Landing Pages: Use AI to change the website text based on who clicks. A manager sees "Boost team ROI," while a student sees "Get a job."
* AI Chatbots: Put a bot on the signup page. It can answer student questions about pricing or syllabus 24/7 to close the sale.

------------------------------
## 3. Key Metrics to Monitor
We need data to know if our fixes are actually working. Here are the core metrics to track:

* Click Through Rate (CTR): Are people actually clicking our ads, or is our creative boring?
* Conversion Rate (CR): Out of 100 people who visit the website, how many actually sign up?
* Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): How much money do we spend on ads to get one paying student?
* Return on Ad Spend (ROAS): For every $1 we put into marketing, how many dollars do we make back?
